Pokémon Guessing Browser Source

Usage:

  • ?channel=YourTwitchName – required to read chat.
  • &clues=6 – number of clues per round.
  • &totaltime=45 – total round time in seconds.
  • &interval=5 – seconds between clues.
  • &reveal=7 – seconds before end when sprite starts revealing.
  • &sync=0 – seconds users can guess before the round starts.
  • &autostart=10 – seconds after a round ends before the next round starts.
  • &debug=true – enables browser console logging.

Example:

https://yourname.github.io/poke-game/?channel=yourchannel&clues=6&totaltime=45&interval=5&reveal=7&sync=0&autostart=10&debug=true
